# Business Insights & Recommendations

**Dataset:** Tableau Sample Superstore (Order dates 2014-01-03 – 2017-12-30)

## Key Metrics
- Total Sales: $2,297,200.86
- Total Profit: $286,397.02
- Profit Margin: 12.47%
- Orders: 5,009
- Customers: 793
- AOV: $458.61

## Insights

1. Overall Performance: The Superstore generated $2,297,201 in sales and $286,397 in profit (12.5% margin) across 5,009 orders from 793 customers. Average Order Value is $458.61.

2. Category Profitability: Technology is the most profitable category. Furniture has high sales but the lowest profit margin (2.5%), largely driven by heavy discounting on Tables and Bookcases.

3. Regional Performance: West is the top-performing region by sales. Focus growth and inventory investments there while investigating under-performing regions for pricing, assortment, or shipping issues.

4. Discount Impact: Discounts above 20% sharply erode margins. Orders with 41-60% discount show significantly negative or near-zero profit margins. Implement stricter discount guardrails and consider value-based promotions instead of deep cuts.

5. Customer Concentration (Pareto): The top 20% of customers generate approximately 48% of total revenue. Prioritize retention and exclusive offers for Champions and Loyal Customers.

6. At-Risk / Need Attention Segments: These customers still represent meaningful revenue (~$665,329). Targeted win-back campaigns (personalized offers, free shipping, loyalty points) can recover high-value relationships at relatively low cost.

7. Shipping Mode: Standard Class dominates volume. Same Day and First Class have higher per-order costs but may support higher-margin Technology products. Optimize shipping rules by product margin and customer segment.

8. Recommended Actions:
   • Protect Champions & Loyalists with VIP programs and early access.
   • Run targeted re-activation for At Risk / Hibernating high-monetary customers.
   • Reduce deep discounts on Furniture (especially Tables/Bookcases); test bundles.
   • Double down on Technology assortment and West region inventory.
   • Monitor AOV and introduce order-value thresholds for free shipping / free gifts.
