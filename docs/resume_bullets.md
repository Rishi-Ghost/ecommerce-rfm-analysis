# Resume Bullet Points – E-commerce Sales Analysis + RFM Segmentation

- Built a complete analytics pipeline (Python/pandas) producing cleaned datasets, RFM-scored customer table, and segment-level KPIs ready for interactive Power BI / Tableau dashboards (Sales Overview, RFM Segmentation, Product Profitability pages).

- Quantified the destructive impact of deep discounting: orders with >20% discount showed sharply declining profit margins, with 41–60% discount bands frequently unprofitable; recommended guardrails and value-based promotions.

- Identified high-value recovery opportunity: “At Risk” and “Need Attention” segments still represent significant revenue (~$665K); recommended targeted win-back campaigns (personalized offers, free shipping, loyalty incentives) to improve retention.

- Delivered 8 prioritized business recommendations covering VIP programs for Champions, inventory focus on West region + Technology category, discount policy reform, and AOV-lifting tactics.
